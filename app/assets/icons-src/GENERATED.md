# icons-src

SVG masters from the Claude Design export
(`design-export/mobile-task-manager-prototype/project/assets/`).

These are reference only. The shipped PNGs in `../images/` are generated by
`app/scripts/generate-app-icons.mjs`, which composes the same glyph from its
tight bounding box (`viewBox 11 9 78 80`) and re-applies padding per platform.

Fills: iOS 82% · Android adaptive 60% · splash 52% · notification 58%.
